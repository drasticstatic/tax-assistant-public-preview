# The expense ledger

*Every tool, service and machine the work runs on. Prices are list prices with the date read, or blank until read; the business share is Christopher's call on each row, and a CPA decides what the return says. Started 2026-11-04 from SOP-ARB-008 (the tech stack), the round-six proposals, and Christopher's own list. Add rows; never erase one, mark it ended.*

## How to read a row

| Column | Means |
|---|---|
| **Kind** | subscription (recurring), one-time (bought once), service (usage-billed), hardware (lasts more than a year: usually depreciated or expensed under section 179 or the de minimis safe harbor, a CPA's call) |
| **Business share** | 100% (only the work uses it), mixed (split; the personal part is not deductible), personal, or **?** (not decided) |
| **Owner** | who paid and who owns it: Christopher, or a foundation if it bought the thing (then it is their asset, not his expense; proposal 016) |
| **Receipt** | where the proof lives (email, App Store, a statement); private paths are named by folder only |
