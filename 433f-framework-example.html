📋 Form 433-F — Fill-In Framework (Example)

A reusable template for anyone preparing for an IRS Collection Information Statement call — every value shown is a placeholder example, not real data.

📋 This is a public, generic example. All institution names and dollar figures shown are placeholders so you can see the shape of the form filled in, then replace them with your own information in your own private copy. Nothing on this page reflects any specific person's real financial data.

☎️ Opening the call — example script

"I'm calling to formally establish Currently Not Collectible status and would like to complete Form 433-F with you directly."

If asked about the origin of a tax balance, state your actual circumstances plainly and factually — the IRS is assessing your current ability to pay, not judging why the balance exists.

🧾 About this framework

  • Mirrors the real IRS Form 433-F sections, in plain language
  • Built and open-sourced by the maintainer while preparing his own CNC application
  • Deliberately excludes any field for SSN, EIN, or full account numbers — build that discipline into your own copy too
  • Cryptocurrency is treated as property, not currency, by the IRS — use fair-market value as of today
